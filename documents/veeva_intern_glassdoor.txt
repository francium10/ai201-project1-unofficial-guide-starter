Title: Veeva Systems Software Engineer Intern Review - Summer 2023

Role: Software Engineer Intern, Commercial Cloud Team
Location: Pleasanton, CA (remote option available)
Rating: 4/5

Pros:
Veeva is genuinely different from Big Pharma in that it's a pure SaaS company selling into life sciences. As an intern you're building real features in production systems used by Pfizer, Merck, AstraZeneca. My project involved building a new data validation module for Vault CRM — it shipped to production before my internship ended. That almost never happens at big pharma internships.

The engineering culture is strong. Code reviews are thorough. They use modern stack — Java microservices, React frontend, AWS. Daily standups, sprint planning, retrospectives — real Agile, not fake Agile.

The Generation Veeva intern program is well-organized. Weekly speaker series where executives come talk to interns. Cohort is ~80 interns. You get a buddy (junior employee) and a mentor (senior engineer). Both were genuinely helpful.

Cons:
Pleasanton is a suburb with not much to do. If you want city life, the remote option is better but you lose some of the cohort experience. 

The interview process is technical and moves fast — two LeetCode-style problems (medium difficulty) plus system design for senior interns. Prepare accordingly. They ask life-sciences-specific questions like "design a clinical trial data tracking system" so domain knowledge helps.

Pay: $45-50/hr for SWE interns. No housing stipend. Relocation reimbursement up to $2,500.

Would recommend for anyone wanting pharma-tech SaaS experience specifically. If you want pure biotech lab work, look elsewhere. If you want software that matters in life sciences, Veeva is excellent.

Interview Tips:
- Know what Vault CRM and Veeva Network are before your interview
- Prepare a story about working with data at scale
- They care a LOT about communication — explain your thinking out loud during technical screens
